What moved, and why these four

Before: Templates ▸ Choose Template… (a whole top-level menu holding one item) · Tools ▸ Tailor to Job ▸ … · Tools ▸ AI Assistant ▸ Show AI Panel · File ▸ Import ▸ LinkedIn Export (.zip).

The template control shows a thumbnail, not just a name. It is the only always-visible reminder that 25 designs exist, and it makes the picker discoverable without opening it. Costs 17×24 px.

AI Assistant carries a status dot, never a disabled state. Green when a local model is reachable, grey when nothing is configured — but the button always opens the panel. CLAUDE.md's rule is that AI features degrade rather than gate; a greyed-out control says "broken" instead. See the AI status strip component.

Zoom, undo and redo move right, next to Save and Export. They were at the far left, ~2800 px from Export on a wide window. Grouping every document-level action on one side means the pointer stops crossing the screen.

Export is the only filled button in the app. One primary action per surface — it is what the user came to do, and nothing else should compete with it for the accent colour.
