Decisions worth arguing with

Thumbnails stay white in both themes. The grid of white pages against dark chrome is intentional — those are documents, not UI. It also means the two renderings above share every pixel inside the thumbnails, which is the point.

Category filter as chips, not a dropdown. The categories already exist in TemplateInfo.Category. Chips expose the taxonomy without a click, and "All 25" doubles as a count — a selling point the current UI never states anywhere.

Search included because 25 is past the scanning threshold. Under ~12 items a grid alone is enough; at 25 and growing, typing "ats" or "academic" beats scanning.

The primary button names the template ("Use Modern", not "OK") — the click that restyles the whole document should say what it will do.

Still open: should selecting a card apply immediately with live preview behind the modal, or only on confirm? Immediate suits the app's live-preview character, but re-rendering the PDF on every arrow-key move through 25 items may be too slow. Worth measuring, not guessing.
