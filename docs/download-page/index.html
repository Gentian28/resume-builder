What it does

◆ Runs the AI locally

Point it at Ollama or LM Studio and summaries, skill suggestions and bullet rewrites all happen on your machine. Nothing is sent anywhere.

◆ 25 templates

From ATS-plain single-column to Europass, federal long-form and photo headers. Your content stays put; only the layout changes.

◆ Tailor to a job ad

Paste a posting for a keyword match score and the terms you are missing. The scoring is local computation and works with no AI configured at all.

◆ Export anywhere

PDF, DOCX, HTML, PNG, plain text and JSON Resume. Import from LinkedIn exports, an existing PDF, or JSON Resume.

◆ No paywall on export

Every builder that makes you pay to download your own résumé is doing so by choice. This one does not.

◆ Your data stays yours

Local SQLite, no account, no telemetry. Optional two-way sync through a folder you choose — Dropbox, OneDrive, or nothing at all.

These builds are not code-signed yet

Windows shows an "unknown publisher" SmartScreen warning — choose More info → Run anyway. macOS Gatekeeper refuses the package until you allow it under System Settings → Privacy & Security.

Every release publishes SHA256SUMS-*.txt so you can verify what you downloaded. A free code-signing certificate through SignPath's open-source programme is planned, which removes both warnings.
